# Add Food Flow Update

The Food page has been simplified into six clear actions inspired by premium calorie-tracking apps:

1. **Describe** — type or speak naturally, for example: `2 rotis, dal and tea for lunch`
2. **Search** — search Eatlyte common foods + USDA + Open Food Facts + optional FatSecret
3. **Photo** — upload a meal photo and review AI estimates before saving
4. **Barcode** — scan or enter packaged-food barcode
5. **Label** — scan or manually enter nutrition label values
6. **Custom** — save homemade or personal food values

Important safety choice: every AI/photo/label result still requires review before saving. This avoids misleading users and keeps the app aligned with wellness guidance rather than medical diagnosis.
