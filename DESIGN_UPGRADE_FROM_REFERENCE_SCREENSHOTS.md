# Premium Theme Upgrade From User Reference Screenshots

## Visual direction implemented
- Removed green brand direction completely.
- Shifted the product to a premium monochrome wellness look: warm white, soft grey/lilac surfaces, black primary actions, and only small semantic macro colors.
- Light mode: clean white/off-white, large rounded cards, pill navigation, black CTA/FAB.
- Dark mode: deep charcoal surfaces instead of pure black everywhere, with high contrast text and muted secondary labels.
- Nutrition colors are used only where they carry meaning:
  - Protein: soft red
  - Carbs: warm amber
  - Fat: calm blue
  - Water: muted blue
  - Alerts: red/amber only for safety states

## Reference features added/adapted
- Premium bottom pill tab bar similar to the screenshots.
- Large black floating add button.
- Minimal black Eatlyte mark instead of colorful logo treatment.
- Camera/photo scanner panel with dark scanner-style UI.
- Exercise entry cards: Run, Weight lifting, Describe, Manual.
- Softer rounded cards and list rows across dashboard, food, exercise, reports, and quick add.
- Simplified premium button system: black primary, soft neutral secondary.

## Product features recommended next
1. Full nutrition detail sheet per food with unit tabs: g, cup, oz, serving.
2. Saved foods and saved meals tabs.
3. True camera scanner screen with modes: Scan Food, Barcode, Food Label.
4. Progress dashboard with weekly energy, weight change, BMI, macros, and photos.
5. Food database result screen with recent foods, plus button, and verified source badges.
6. Exercise logging flow with Run, Weight lifting, Describe, and Manual pages.
7. Premium onboarding that asks goal, diet type, allergies, cuisine, family members, and activity level.
8. Subscription-ready Pro screens: weekly reports, smart meal plan, family plan, grocery list.

## Safety notes
Nutrition and exercise estimates must remain wellness guidance only. The app should avoid diagnosis or condition-specific treatment advice and should route chronic disease, pregnancy, child nutrition, elderly nutrition, medication, eating disorder, kidney, heart, or diabetes concerns to a clinician or registered dietitian.
