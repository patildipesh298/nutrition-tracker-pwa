# Eatlyte Logo Update

This build replaces the earlier Eatlyte logo with a cleaner, more professional symbol-first identity.

## Logo concept

The mark combines four ideas that match the product:

- **Circular progress ring**: daily nutrition tracking and macro progress.
- **Bowl/plate base**: meals and food logging.
- **Fork detail**: everyday eating and practical meal tracking.
- **Coral/amber food shapes**: balanced nutrition without making the brand look medical or overly technical.

## Usage decision

The app header now uses the logo mark only. This keeps the mobile UI cleaner and avoids repeating the wordmark in cramped spaces. The full `logo.svg` lockup is still included for marketing, landing pages, social previews, and future app-store creative.
